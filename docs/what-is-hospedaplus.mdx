# What is HospedaPlus?

<Hero>

HospedaPlus is a data-driven platform designed to help hospitality businesses make better decisions through analytics and automation.

We collect, transform and analyze data from multiple sources to provide actionable insights on occupancy, revenue, customer behavior and operational performance. Our goal is to simplify data access, improve efficiency and support growth in the hospitality industry.
