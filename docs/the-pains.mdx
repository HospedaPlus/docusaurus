# The Pains

The hospitality industry faces several challenges when it comes to data, operations and decision-making. These are the main pains we aim to solve with HospedaPlus.

<CardGrid columns={3}>
  <InfoCard icon="database" title="Fragmented data">
    Information is spread across multiple systems and formats, making it hard to get a complete view.
  </InfoCard>
  <InfoCard icon="clock" title="Slow decision-making">
    Reports are often manual and delayed, which limits the ability to react to changes in real time.
  </InfoCard>
  <InfoCard icon="line-chart" title="Unclear guest behavior">
    Lack of consolidated insights makes it difficult to understand guest preferences and improve their experience.
  </InfoCard>
  <InfoCard icon="gear" title="Operational inefficiencies">
    Without data-driven processes, resources are not always used in the best way.
  </InfoCard>
  <InfoCard icon="alert" title="Revenue loss">
    Missed opportunities due to inaccurate forecasting and poor segmentation.
  </InfoCard>
  <InfoCard icon="shield" title="Limited scalability">
    Manual and isolated systems cannot easily grow with the business.
  </InfoCard>
</CardGrid>
