# The Pains

HospedaPlus has no single view of its room inventory or of the booking pace for each stay date, so prices are adjusted too late. These are the main pains the platform must solve.

<CardGrid columns={3}>
  <InfoCard icon="x-circle" title="High cancellation rate">
    37% of bookings are cancelled, and there is no cancellation policy differentiated by channel.
  </InfoCard>
  <InfoCard icon="alert" title="Uncontrolled overbooking">
    Hotels overbook without control, so guests have to be relocated, at a high cost and with bad reviews.
  </InfoCard>
  <InfoCard icon="clock" title="Late, manual pricing">
    Prices are adjusted by hand with a one-week delay.
  </InfoCard>
  <InfoCard icon="percent" title="Commissions not deducted">
    Channel commissions are not deducted when revenue is measured, so the real income per channel is unknown.
  </InfoCard>
  <InfoCard icon="message" title="Unanalyzed negative reviews">
    Negative reviews are not analyzed systematically.
  </InfoCard>
  <InfoCard icon="database" title="No single view of bookings">
    Bookings arrive from a channel manager, agency files and the own booking engine, with no unified view of inventory or pickup.
  </InfoCard>
</CardGrid>
