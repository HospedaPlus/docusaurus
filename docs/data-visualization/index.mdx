# Data Visualization

**HospedaPlus Revenue** is the Power BI report the Revenue Manager and the hotel managers use to decide on prices, occupancy and sales channels. It is the last step of the platform: it **only reads the gold layer** of the S3 lakehouse through Amazon Athena, and writes nothing back.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Report | `HospedaPlus_Revenue` (Power BI project, `.pbip` format) |
| Repository | `HospedaPlus/powerbi-hospedaplus` |
| Published in | Power BI Service, refreshed automatically every day at 05:00 Colombia time |
| Open the report | [HospedaPlus_Revenue in Power BI](https://app.powerbi.com/groups/me/reports/7e20788a-a93d-4598-8c59-927aefe49653) (requires signing in with an account that has access) |
| Data source | Athena, database `hospedaplus_gold` (15 tables), via the Amazon Athena ODBC driver |
| Currency | Colombian pesos (COP), converted in silver with the daily exchange rate (F7) |

`gold_modelo_daily` rebuilds every gold table once a night (about 03:35 UTC, 22:35 Colombia time). The report refreshes at 05:00 Colombia time, so every morning it shows the previous night's data. See [DAGs and schedule](../data-engineering-pipeline/dags.mdx).

## Business questions it answers

| # | Question | Main KPI | Report page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **P1** Cancellations | Which channels and lead times concentrate cancellations, and how much revenue is lost? | Cancellation rate · Lost revenue | Executive summary |
| **P2** Overbooking | How many rooms are rejected or relocated for lack of availability? | Rejection rate · Relocated rooms | Executive summary · Performance by hotel |
| **P3** Price and demand | What price to charge based on pickup, season and city events? | Suggested price vs. current rate · Pickup vs. last year | Prices and demand |
| **P4** Channels | How much does each channel cost and which leaves the most net revenue? | Effective commission · Net revenue | Executive summary · Cost by channel |
| **P5** Reputation | How is each hotel's reputation and how does it relate to occupancy and RevPAR? | Rating (RN-10) · % positive reviews | Executive summary · Performance by hotel |

How each pain is solved in the data is explained in [How each pain is solved](../pain-resolution.mdx).

## Report pages

The report is in Spanish (page names translated in parentheses). It has a desktop layout (1280 × 720) and a mobile layout for all four pages.

| Page | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Inicio** (Home) | What HospedaPlus is, the goal of the dashboard, where the data comes from, how to use it and the size of the chain |
| **Preguntas de negocio** (Business questions) | P1–P5 with their main KPI and the page where each one is analysed |
| **Resumen ejecutivo** (Executive summary) | Filters (date, country, hotel, channel), KPIs, occupancy by month, cancellation by lead time, cost by channel and performance by hotel. Past dates only |
| **Precios y demanda** (Prices and demand) | Suggested price vs. rate by day, season, hotel and room type; pickup and its period |
