# KPIs and measures

All measures live in the `_Medidas` table of the model, grouped in folders: Occupancy, Revenue, Capacity, Bookings, Channels, Reputation, Pricing and Portfolio.

| KPI (measure) | Formula | Gold source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Occupancy %** | Rooms sold ÷ rooms available | `fact_ocupacion_diaria` |
| **ADR** (average daily rate) | Room revenue ÷ rooms sold | `fact_ocupacion_diaria` |
| **RevPAR** | Room revenue ÷ rooms available | `fact_ocupacion_diaria` |
| **Cancellation Rate** | Cancelled bookings ÷ bookings | `fact_reservas` |
| **Lost Revenue** | Σ max(0, total − penalty) over cancelled bookings | `fact_reservas` |
| **Rejected Rooms** / **Rejection Rate** | Rooms rejected for lack of availability ÷ (sold + rejected) | `fact_ocupacion_diaria` |
| **Relocated Rooms** | Rooms relocated to another hotel | `fact_ocupacion_diaria` |
| **Gross / Net Revenue**, **Commission** | Total, net and commission of **non-cancelled** bookings | `fact_reservas` |
| **Effective Commission %** | Commission ÷ gross revenue | `fact_reservas` |
| **Avg Suggested Price** / **Avg Current Rate** | Average suggested price (rules RN-05/RN-06) and average current rate | `mart_p3_precio_sugerido` |
| **Suggested vs Current %** | (Σ suggested price − Σ rate) ÷ Σ rate | `mart_p3_precio_sugerido` |
| **Pickup %** | Rooms booked 14 days before each night vs. the same night last year (364 days). Only nights with last-year data whose 14-day window has closed | `mart_p3_revenue` |
| **Pickup Period** | Range of nights included in Pickup % | `mart_p3_revenue` |
| **Guest Rating** (RN-10) | Average rating (1–5) at the latest cutoff date | `mart_p5_reputacion` |
| **% Positive** | Positive reviews ÷ all reviews | `fact_resenas` |

## What each KPI means

Hotels measure themselves with a few standard numbers. In plain words:

- **Occupancy %**: out of every 100 rooms the hotel could sell on a night, how many it actually sold. 80 rooms sold of 100 = 80 %.
- **ADR (Average Daily Rate)**: the average price paid per room sold per night. If 10 rooms brought COP 2,000,000, the ADR is COP 200,000. It tells you *how expensive* you sell.
- **RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room)**: room revenue divided by *all* rooms, sold or empty. It joins price and occupancy in one number (RevPAR = ADR × Occupancy). A hotel can raise ADR and still lose RevPAR if it empties too many rooms; that is why RevPAR is the main revenue KPI.
- **Cancellation Rate**: out of every 100 bookings, how many were cancelled or were no-shows.
- **Lost Revenue**: money that cancelled bookings would have brought, minus any penalty the guest paid.
- **Rejected Rooms / Rejection Rate**: rooms guests wanted but could not book because the hotel was full. It measures demand the hotel turned away.
- **Relocated Rooms**: guests moved to another hotel because of overbooking (sold more rooms than existed).
- **Gross Revenue**: what guests paid. **Commission**: what the sales channel (OTA, agency) kept. **Net Revenue**: what is left for the hotel (gross − commission).
- **Effective Commission %**: share of gross revenue that went to channels in commissions.
- **Suggested Price / Current Rate**: the price the pipeline recommends for an upcoming night versus the price published today. **Suggested vs Current %** says how far apart they are.
- **Pickup %**: how fast rooms are being booked compared with last year. Positive means demand is ahead of last year at the same point in time.
- **Guest Rating**: average score that guests give in reviews (1 to 5). **% Positive**: share of reviews with a score of 4 or 5.

## Why "past dates only"

`dim_fecha` reaches one year into the future, and future nights still have few bookings. Mixing them with past nights lowers occupancy artificially (13.4 % instead of 21.7 % in the snapshot below). That is why the Executive summary and the P1, P2, P4 and P5 cards filter `dim_fecha[periodo] = "Ocurrido"` (occurred). P3 does use future nights, because it is about upcoming prices.
